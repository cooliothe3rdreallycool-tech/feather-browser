# Feather

An ultra-light Chromium browser for Windows, about 1.5 MB, with a Pi-hole-style ad blocker (YouTube included), built-in AI assistants, a password locker, tab islands, and themes.

## What Feather does

- **Ad blocker, Pi-hole style:** 70,000+ ad and tracker domains blocked on every page, YouTube ads removed, and leftover ad boxes hidden (EasyList). A shield in the address bar shows what was blocked.
- **Light on your PC:** about 1.5 MB to download. Tabs freeze when you leave them and sleep after 15 minutes, and only your last tab loads when Feather starts. With 16 busy sites open it used about half the memory of the same engine without these.
- **Tabs your way:** tab islands (coloured groups), drag tabs between windows, tab search (Ctrl+Shift+A), and hover cards.
- **AI built in:** a side panel with Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Grok or DeepSeek.
- **Password locker:** your logins encrypted behind one master password, filled in with one click.
- **Bookmarks:** Ctrl+D, and import from Edge, Chrome or Firefox.
- **Looks good:** 8 themes plus any accent colour, tabs in the title bar, and Windows 11 snap layouts.
- **Keyboard and screen readers:** F6 and Alt+Shift+T reach every tab and button, and Narrator reads the tabs out.
- **Safe by default:** HTTPS-only mode, a pop-up blocker, private windows, and signed updates.

## Download

Get the latest version from **[Releases](https://github.com/cooliothe3rdreallycool-tech/feather-browser/releases/latest)**. Feather keeps itself up to date from there.

Every official update is signed with the author's private key. Feather checks that signature before it installs anything and refuses updates that aren't signed.
